## Assessment before new numerical outputs

1. The requested contribution distinction is justified. Posterior-probability
   ranking, comparison-pair accounting and positive-affine invariance are known
   ranking principles. The two-state identity is a truth-dependent diagnostic;
   numerical reconstruction alone is not independent mechanism evidence.
   The contribution must identify the operational evaluation protocol and
   concrete assessment changes rather than advertise a new general theorem.
2. Broader empirical relevance remains only partly tested by OLS and Study C.
   A bounded PELT/segment-VAR adapter will replace recurrent K-means grouping
   and the gate, choose a penalised segment count, and refit nonoverlapping
   intervals. Its window-score input remains shared and its graph adapter is
   ours; this is not a full reproduction of a published causal method or an
   unrestricted generalisation study. The gate is kept as an implementation
   case. Stationary-reference gate calibration is not needed to support a
   general gate claim, because no such claim will be made.
3. The boundary concerns are correct. A fixed raw-amplitude threshold is not
   scale invariant. Standard AP includes the final zero-score group whereas
   positive-change AP abstains there. Both AP definitions and a fixed quantile
   grid will be retained. Method-specific quantiles are descriptive amplitude
   sensitivity, not independent equal-false-alarm calibration. No test-label
   threshold optimisation or best-F1 selection is permitted.
4. The local complete archive exists and has passed retained-output checks.
   That does not prove it was uploaded to any review system, nor does it imply
   fresh fitting was independently rerun. A small, explicitly selected fresh
   generation/fitting check will address the second reproducibility level.
   Public deposition/upload remains outside this local authorised work.
5. Positive residual weight breaks within-group ties. Exact pair-credit
   accounting will separate same-group and cross-group gains at every fixed
   weight, plus one diagnostic near-zero weight; it is not a deployable choice
   rule and is not an additional independent experiment.
6. The four prior audit findings will be repaired: isolated execution-record
   directories in reproduction examples, point-estimate wording for C cells
   whose intervals cross zero, distinct support/coefficient notation, and the
   literal exclamation-mark typo. Official bibliography metadata will be
   checked; unsupported or inaccessible entries will be marked unverified.
