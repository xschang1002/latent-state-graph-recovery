All $300$ planned schedule cells succeed, with $50$ evaluable repeats
per cell. The longer records contain repeated transitions: fixed-phase
$D=300/600$ has eleven/five window-centre switches, compared with three/one
in Study A. Figure~\ref{fig:schedule} reports realised mean counts and mean
within-repeat purity; the archive's evidence index locates the complete
6,900-row window record (Supplement Section~S16).
Nominal duration is not a complete description of the realised schedule.

At $D=600$, forced hard-minus-raw is $+0.072$ $[0.055,0.089]$ for fixed phase,
$+0.072$ $[0.050,0.094]$ for random phase, and $+0.070$ $[0.047,0.091]$
for irregular runs. All three pass the secondary six-test Holm correction.
At $D=300$, estimates are $-0.025$, $+0.003$ and $-0.019$ respectively;
all their pointwise intervals cross zero and none passes Holm6. This does not
establish equivalence to raw. The directly paired duration differences are
$+0.097$ $[0.066,0.129]$, $+0.070$ $[0.039,0.098]$ and
$+0.089$ $[0.057,0.121]$; all pass the separate secondary Holm3 family.
The positive candidate effects therefore persist in these random-phase and
irregular schedules, while remaining conditional on the tested simulator,
windows and scorer.

Native output tells a different operational story. Gate acceptance is only
$2/50$, $0/50$, $3/50$ at $D=300$ and $6/50$, $6/50$, $7/50$ at $D=600$
for fixed, random and irregular schedules. All native hard-minus-raw estimates
are negative, from $-0.214$ to $-0.198$ (Figure~\ref{fig:gate}).
The candidate's positive state-discrimination gain at $D=600$ is therefore
not a positive gain of the native constant-fallback pipeline. The raw-fallback
control in Section~\ref{sec:control-results} separates the rejection output's
information loss from which candidates the gate accepts.

Table~\ref{tab:factors} compares all forced candidates on their common
evaluable repeats. At $D=600$, mean per-repeat products are about
$0.666$, $0.656$ and $0.659$, compared with $0.358$, $0.400$ and $0.363$
at $D=300$. State discrimination and contrast concordance change jointly;
the algebra locates the realised performance, but does not identify separate
causal contributions or yield a truth-free rule for selecting a duration.
