Dear Editors of Information Sciences,

I submit “State Aggregation in Dynamic Graph Recovery: Separating Structural
Ranking from Temporal Discrimination” for consideration as an original research
article. The paper studies how replacing local graph estimates by shared
state-level summaries changes recovery of edges at the times they are active.
Its evaluation protocol separates pooled structural ranking, within-edge state
discrimination and boundary detection on the same inputs and local scores.

The contribution combines task-specific diagnostic accounting with controlled
empirical evidence. A 100-repeat paired study finds hard-minus-raw within-edge temporal
AUROC differences of -0.069 and +0.038 at two duration configurations.
A separate 50-repeat schedule study and a separately specified 30-repeat
symmetric-generator/window study bound the conditions under which the contrast
occurs. Duration also changes switch coverage, so the findings remain
configuration-specific.

Fixed score controls show that pooled-ranking gains can occur without changed
temporal order. Residual pair accounting distinguishes restored within-group
ordering from cross-group reordering. Reporting both standard and positive-change
AP with fixed threshold references changes the assessment of boundary recovery
on the same predictions. A PELT plus segment-VAR comparator applies the protocol
to another partition/refit construction: pooled ranking improves, while neither
duration has an established within-edge temporal AUROC advantage. The prototype gate
is one implementation case. The scope remains the tested two-state linear
systems and local linear scorers.

These distinctions are relevant to information aggregation and the evaluation
of data-driven structure recovery. A separate supplement presents definitions,
procedures, derivations, study designs and results. The accompanying archive
contains complete retained simulation inputs and fitted outputs, analysis code
and reproduction instructions. Eight selected same-seed pipelines were also
generated and fitted afresh, with all 492 recorded comparisons exact in the
recorded environment. The industrial example supplies static expert truth only.

This work has not been formally published and is not under consideration
elsewhere. AI assistance in research code and manuscript preparation is disclosed.
I retain responsibility for the scientific content and submission.
