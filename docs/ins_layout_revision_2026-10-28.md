This presentation release does not constitute a journal submission.

## Scope

The revision follows the author-supplied INS layout handoff and starts from
the formal 26-page main article and 43-page supplement. The revised main article
has 26 pages, five numbered figures and three numbered tables; the supplement
has 45 pages, eleven numbered figures and 33 numbered tables. This is the chosen
layout target, not a claim about how the journal counts every supplementary
panel. Experimental inputs, simulations, seeds, fitted arrays, statistical
tests, bootstrap streams, missingness conventions and scientific conclusions
are unchanged. The abstract, introduction, AI disclosure and author details
are preserved.

## Supplement display changes

The S1--S8 organisation is preserved. The B schedule figure and the complete
A/B factor table sit in S4.1. The former dense boundary-sensitivity table is
split into AP (Table S12) and F1 (Table S13), each retaining all 18 configurations. Undefined
targets, denominators and interval endpoints are retained. Current table and
figure numbers are generated by LaTeX; dated evidence indices retain their
historical numbering and are interpreted through the current research guide.

## Reproduction and access

The release's complete `information_sciences_reproducibility.zip` contains the revised
manuscript, supplement, editable sources, display builder and presentation
manifest. The companion submission-files ZIP includes both revised PDFs and
standalone flat source ZIPs. The SHA-256 file identifies these artifacts.
The [versioned release](https://github.com/xschang1002/latent-state-graph-recovery/releases/tag/v2026.10.28-layout.1)
provides the current archive and PDFs. Earlier public releases retain their
preceding presentations.

Read-only verification remains `python -B scripts/reproduce_paper.py --verify`.
For presentation-only rebuilding, use
`python -B tools/build_narrative_assets.py` in a separate working copy. The
broader historical `--rebuild` route has a different scope, as described in the
artifact README. Neither route denotes new independent scientific evidence.
