## Official sources and access

The [KBS official description](https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/knowledge-based-systems/0950-7051)
was directly readable. Its scope includes machine-learning and data-science
methodology, with theoretical and empirical contributions. This supports
positioning the article around evaluation and interpretation of learned
relational structure. It does not support inventing a new algorithm or a
validated decision-support application.

The [KBS Guide for Authors](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/knowledge-based-systems/publish/guide-for-authors)
and [ScienceDirect aims and scope](https://www.sciencedirect.com/journal/knowledge-based-systems/about/aims-and-scope)
returned HTTP 403. The browser fallback was not permitted to use Edge and
stopped before navigation. Third-party guide copies and the previous INS
requirements were not used to fill the missing KBS-specific instructions.
